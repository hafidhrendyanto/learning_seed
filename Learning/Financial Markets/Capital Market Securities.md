# Capital Market Securities

Capital market securities are **long-term securities** with maturities **greater than one year** or no maturity at all.

## Key Characteristics
- Longer time horizon
- Higher potential returns than money market securities
- Higher risk (interest rate risk, credit risk, market risk)
- Essential for funding long-term investments like infrastructure, business expansion, and home purchases

## Types of Capital Market Securities

### Bonds
- Long-term debt securities
- Provide periodic interest payments
- Repay principal at maturity
- Types: Treasury bonds, corporate bonds, municipal bonds

### Mortgages
- **Residential Mortgages** — Finance home purchases, require creditworthiness assessment
- **Commercial Mortgages** — Finance commercial properties (offices, malls, warehouses)
- **Mortgage-Backed Securities (MBS)** — Claims on a package of bundled mortgages, created through securitization

### Stocks
- Represent partial ownership in corporations
- Returns come from dividends and capital gains
- No maturity date
- Liquidity depends on firm size and trading volume
