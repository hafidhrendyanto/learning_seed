# Money Market Securities

Money market securities are **short-term debt securities** with a maturity of **one year or less**.

## Key Characteristics
- **High liquidity** — can be easily converted to cash
- **Low credit risk** — issuers are typically governments or highly rated corporations
- **Short maturity** — minimizes interest rate risk and default risk

## Types of Money Market Securities

- **Treasury Bills (T-Bills)** — Issued by the U.S. Treasury to fund government operations
- **Commercial Paper** — Unsecured short-term debt issued by corporations
- **Negotiable Certificates of Deposit (CDs)** — Issued by depository institutions, can be traded in secondary markets

## Why They Matter

Money market securities serve as a safe place for surplus units to park excess funds temporarily while still earning some return. They are the "cash equivalent" of the investment world.
