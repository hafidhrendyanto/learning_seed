# Equity Securities

Equity securities represent ownership in a firm.

## Key Characteristics

- Confer **partial ownership** in a corporation
- Returns come from **dividends** and **capital gains**
- No legal obligation for the company to pay dividends
- No maturity date — ownership lasts until sold

## Examples

- Common stocks
- Preferred stocks

## Types of Markets for Equity

Equity securities are primarily traded in **capital markets** (long-term markets) rather than money markets, since stocks have no maturity date and represent long-term ownership.

## Why Issue Equity?

**Advantages for issuer:**
- No obligation to make periodic payments
- No maturity — never needs to be repaid
- Reduces financial risk during low-cash-flow periods

**Disadvantages for issuer:**
- Dilutes ownership and control
- Existing shareholders own a smaller percentage

## Why Investors Prefer Equity

- Potential for higher returns than debt
- Participation in company growth
- Voting rights (common stock)

## Treasury Stock (Buybacks)

Companies can **buy back their own shares** from the [[Secondary Market|secondary market]]. These repurchased shares become **treasury stock**.

### Characteristics of Treasury Stock
- Does **not** receive dividends
- Usually has **no voting rights**
- Reduces **shares outstanding**
- Can be **reissued** later or used for employee stock compensation

### Why Companies Buy Back Shares
- **Return cash to shareholders** (alternative to dividends)
- **Signal confidence** that the stock is undervalued
- **Boost EPS** (earnings per share) by reducing the denominator
- **Offset dilution** from employee stock options
- **Prevent hostile takeovers** by reducing shares available

### Caution
- Buybacks only create value if the company doesn't **overpay**
- Money spent on buybacks could instead fund R&D or expansion
- Can signal a lack of profitable investment opportunities

## Comparison with Debt

| Feature | [[Debt Securities]] | Equity Securities |
|---------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Ownership | No | Yes |
| Periodic Payments | Required (interest) | Optional (dividends) |
| Maturity | Fixed | No maturity |
| Risk to Investor | Lower | Higher |
| Risk to Issuer | Default risk | Dilution risk |
